What does it take to be a sandwich?

Two or more slices of bread:The traditional requirement.
A split roll or bun: A single piece of bread cut open but still connected at the seam (like a sub or hoagie).
Flatbread wrappers: Pita pockets or folded flatbreads that enclose a filling.
🧀 The Inner FillingProteins: Sliced deli meats, grilled chicken, bacon, fish, meatballs, egg salad, or tofu.Cheeses: Sliced or melted varieties like cheddar, Swiss, provolone, or American.Vegetables: Crisp layers of lettuce, tomato slices, onions, pickles, or peppers.Spreads & Condiments: Mayonnaise, mustard, butter, oil and vinegar, or relish to add moisture and flavor.
